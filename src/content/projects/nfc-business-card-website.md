A physical business card, with a web experience one NFC tap away. I designed and developed the site connecting the two.

> **Prototyped for an audience of 50+.** The service later expanded into other NFC-based experiences.

### The tap is only the beginning

Behind the card were **profile pages, onboarding, and an admin area**. I built the paths people needed to set up a profile and reach it through their card.

### Send people to the right place

Server-authenticated redirects connected those pages. The project brought together the visible profile and the account flows needed to support it.

### Built with

React · TypeScript · AWS
